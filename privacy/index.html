Melodi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Melodi mobile app (“the app”) handles and how. The app is developed and published by [developer name on Google Play] (“we”). Questions can be sent to destek@example.com.

In short

  • No accounts. No ad networks, analytics or tracking. We do not sell, rent or use your personal data for advertising.
  • Your songs, recordings, the melodies found, your practice and your streak stay on your phone only. We have no server and cannot see them.
  • The microphone listens only when you turn on “Listen” or “Listen to my piano”. The sound is processed on the phone and is never sent anywhere.
  • “Find on YouTube” opens Google's YouTube website. What you search and watch there, and what you type in the search box, goes to Google under Google's terms.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes everything.

1. Data that stays on your phone

The following is stored only in the app's own private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot reach. It is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • copies of the audio files you add, and the sound of songs recorded with “Find on YouTube” and “Listen”,
  • the melodies the app finds and any corrections you make to them,
  • your practice time, streak, streak shields, best results and where you left off in each song,
  • your settings (including the reminder time) and your recent searches (at most 8).

If Android's backup is turned on on your phone, Android may include the app's settings and data in the backup in your Google account. That backup is a Google service; we cannot access it.

2. Microphone

Microphone permission is asked for only when you first tap the related button or option, and only for two things:

  • Listen: to hear a song playing nearby. What is heard is saved on your phone as a song and its melody is found on the phone.
  • Listen to my piano: to hear the note you play on a real piano. Nothing is recorded; the sound is processed in the moment and let go.

Sound from the microphone never leaves the phone. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

3. YouTube

“Find on YouTube” opens YouTube's mobile website (m.youtube.com) inside the app. That page is served by Google. When you use it, YouTube and Google collect data under their own terms, for example the videos you search for and watch, cookies, device information and your IP address. The YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

The app shows that page fitted to the app (it hides YouTube's own menus). When you tap “Extract the melody”, the video plays on your phone as usual and the app records the sound as it plays, on the phone. That sound is kept on your phone only, to find the melody and to play the song while you practise, and is never sent anywhere. Adverts are not recorded.

Cookies YouTube leaves on your phone through this page are deleted when you clear the app's data.

4. Search suggestions and video pictures

What you type in the search box is sent to Google's suggestion service (suggestqueries.google.com) to show suggestions as you type, and to YouTube when you search. Pictures of songs added from YouTube are loaded from YouTube's image server (i.ytimg.com). These requests reach Google with your phone's IP address and technical details, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. We do not see or keep what you type. Your recent searches are kept only on your phone and can be removed from the suggestion list.

5. Reminder notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it uses the phone's own alarm and notification services. The reminder is scheduled and shown on your phone and does not involve any server. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in your phone's notification settings.

6. When you write to us

“Write to us” and “Wrong melody? Report it” open the e-mail app on your phone. Nothing is sent until you send it, and you see everything in the message first. A message that reaches us contains your e-mail address, what you wrote and technical lines added below it: the app version, Android version and phone model. A melody report also includes the song's name, the second you were at and, for songs from YouTube, the video's link. You can delete these lines before sending.

We use them only to answer you and fix the problem, never share them, and delete them within 12 months after the matter is closed, or at once if you ask.

7. Rating the app

The app may now and then open Google Play's own rating window. Your rating and review go to Google Play under Google's terms. The app never sees what you gave or wrote.

8. Who data is shared with

Only in the cases described above, only with Google (YouTube, search suggestions, Google Play), and only for the app to work. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. Data such as general crash statistics that Google Play collects from Android is handled by Google under its own terms, independently of the app.

9. Security

Data on your phone is kept in Android's private storage for the app. The app's internet connections (YouTube and Google) are encrypted with HTTPS.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it. Swipe a song left in the list to delete it; its sound and melody are deleted together.
  • To delete everything: in your phone's settings, Apps > Melodi > Storage > Clear data. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.
  • There are no accounts, so there is no account to delete.
  • To have e-mails you sent us deleted, write to the address above.

11. Children

Melodi is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. YouTube's own age rules apply to using YouTube.

12. Your rights

You control the data on your phone; we cannot see it. To exercise your rights of access, correction, deletion and objection regarding e-mails you sent us, write to us. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country, under the GDPR in Europe or Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data in Türkiye.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes. Significant changes are also announced in the app.
